# Privacy policy

**English** | [Português (Brasil)](PRIVACY.pt-BR.md)

This policy covers the CK Karaoke apps for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and
Linux. CK Karaoke is an unofficial remote control. It is not made by, endorsed
by or affiliated with the makers of Videoke VSK, CooKara or RafKara units.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it sends
no data to the developer or to any other company.

## What stays on your device

- **Favorites.** Your favorite folders are stored only on the phone or computer
  where you made them. The Android app excludes them from Google's automatic
  backup.
- **The song list.** The app copies the song list from your karaoke unit and
  keeps that copy on your device.
- **Settings**, such as the app language and the address of your unit.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

## Network use

The app talks only to the karaoke unit on your local network. It finds the unit
by a broadcast on that network, then sends it the commands you choose. The app
runs a small web server inside itself to show its screens. That server accepts
connections only from the same device, never from the network.

When you tap a YouTube link, the app opens YouTube in your browser or in the
YouTube app. From then on, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

## Camera

The app uses the camera only to read a favorites QR code from another device's
screen, and only after you open the scanner and allow it. Images are processed
on your device. They are not saved and not sent anywhere.

## Sharing and backup

When you share a favorites folder by QR code, the code holds only that folder's
name and song numbers. When you save a backup, the file goes where you choose,
such as your device's storage or a cloud drive. That service's privacy policy
then applies to the file.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page, and its history is kept in
the repository. For questions, open an issue at
<https://github.com/rrgmc/ck-karaoke/issues>.
